One Figure, Multiple Aspect Ratios

A single reference diagram, rearranged for landscape, square, and portrait canvases.

Abstract

Scientific figures are often designed for a single paper column, slide, poster, or webpage. Reusing the same pipeline diagram in a new canvas usually requires tedious manual editing, and image-based resizing frequently distorts text, breaks relationships, or wastes space. We study the problem of automatically relayouting pipeline diagrams so that one source figure can be adapted to many target aspect ratios while retaining semantic structure and visual readability.

Our method uses an agentic relayout pipeline that reasons over diagram components, relationships, and canvas constraints, producing fully editable results rather than flattened screenshots. Across challenging examples, it preserves graph relationships, avoids hallucinated components, and supports standard as well as extreme aspect ratios.

Contributions

  • Canvas-aware relayout. Transforms pipeline diagrams into landscape, square, portrait, cinema, and tall formats while adapting their composition to the target canvas.
  • Editable outputs. Produces diagram layouts for downstream editing workflows in papers, posters, and presentations.
  • Baseline comparison. Evaluates against multimodal and diagram-editing baselines across ten examples, examining relationships, hallucination, and layout quality.
